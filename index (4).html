Youa Vang

Gen AI Artist·Creative Director·Visual Development Artist

I'm Youa Vang, a visual development artist and creative professional currently exploring the cutting edge of generative AI. I work with Oxen.ai, testing the latest image and video generation models and creating tutorials that explore their capabilities and creative potential. I bring over 25 years of experience in animation, film, and visual storytelling, and I'm also an instructor at ArtCenter College of Design. My passion is bringing together art, storytelling, and technology to push the boundaries of what's creatively possible.

Most of my professional AI-generated work is under NDA right now. The films below are mostly competition entries, each made in 48 hours to 4 days, and they are a small sampling of the work I do. They were made on short deadlines, so they do not have the finish of a final product, but they show my taste, my design and my storytelling.
